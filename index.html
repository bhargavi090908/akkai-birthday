I don't expect us to be perfect. I just want us to keep sharing our lives, laughing at stupid things, annoying each other, celebrating each other's wins, and making new memories even as we grow older.

Because no matter how many people come into our lives, you'll always be the sister I grew up with, the person I learned from, and my first teacher in so many little ways.
